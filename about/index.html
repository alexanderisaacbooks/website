About

Alexander Isaac is a UK writer of science fiction, psychological and supernatural thrillers.

The books tend to start somewhere ordinary, such as a records office, a budget meeting or a coroner’s file, and follow it somewhere it shouldn’t go.

How the books are made

Each book is planned, directed and edited by Alexander Isaac and drafted with the help of AI. The final say on every page is his.
